The first release of **TaskBarGame: Harvest Edition**: Skyblock-style farming above your Windows taskbar.

Download **TaskBarGame-HarvestEdition.exe** below and run it (Windows 10/11, 64-bit, nothing to install).
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed yet: click
**More info → Run anyway**. From now on the game updates itself: it tells you when a new version is out.

## The game

- **One long row of crops.** Your farmer walks it breaking every grown crop, then takes the teleport pad
  back to the start. Crops regrow in a few seconds, so the harvest never stops.
- **Farming and Garden levels**, ten crops to unlock, collections and a longer row to buy.
- **Hoes** for every crop, **armour** sets and **talismans**, all crafted from packed crops.
- The **Bazaar**: buy and sell with other (pretend) farmers, with sell offers and buy orders.

## Garden

- **Visitors** arrive every 15 minutes and trade crops for coins, XP and Garden Tokens.
- The **Visitor Shop**: boosts, compost, benches for more visitors, and Wardrobe items.
- The **Composter** turns spare crops into Compost for permanent upgrades and fertiliser.
- A **tool for every crop** (sickle, dicer, shovel...) in iron, gold and blue, swung by the farmer.

## Quality of life

- Click and drag along the row to farm by hand.
- The **Compactor** packs crops as they're harvested.
- Choose how many crops to pack, which crops auto-sell, and lock items so they're never sold.
- Selling to the Market keeps what your next upgrades need.
- **Buy missing** gets an upgrade's missing ingredients from the Bazaar in one click.
- A pop-up and a number on the Upgrades tab when you can craft something new.
- Bazaar search, your own prices for orders, and a price chart.
- Coins an hour for every crop, and each crop's max collection.
- Choose which pop-ups you see, short numbers, and a battery saver.
- The panel opens where you left it.
- **Automatic updates** (it asks first, or installs by itself if you turn that on).

## Taskbar buddy

- **Keyboard shortcuts** from any app: Ctrl+Alt+F opens the panel, Ctrl+Alt+H hides the strip (both
  changeable).
- Put the strip on **any monitor**.
- A **Wardrobe** of hats and outfits for your farmer, unlocked by milestones.
- **Right now:** how many coins, crops and Farming XP an hour your farm is making.
- **Backups** every couple of hours, plus export and import.

Your save is kept separately from the original TaskBarGame, so you can play both.
