The first playable version: **Farming and the Garden**.

## Download

- **TaskBarGame.exe**: Windows 10/11 (64-bit). Just run it, nothing to install.
- **TaskBarGame-win-x64.zip**: the same file, zipped, in case your browser blocks `.exe` downloads.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed yet. Click
**More info → Run anyway**.

## What's in it

- A garden strip above your taskbar. Your farmer sweeps side to side along the row with their hoe,
  breaking every ripe crop they pass.
- Click ripe crops to harvest them yourself (+25 Harvest Fortune), click growing crops to tend them,
  right-click a plot to choose its crop.
- 10 crops, Farming skill (levels 0-50), Garden levels (1-15) that unlock crops and up to 12 plots,
  collections with 10 tiers per crop, and a Market to sell crops.
- The farmer keeps working while the game is closed (half speed, up to 12 hours).
- Settings: always show the strip or only when you hover over the farmer, Small/Normal/Large,
  Left/Center/Right, start with Windows, hide during fullscreen apps.

Your progress is saved in `%APPDATA%\TaskBarGame\save.json`.
